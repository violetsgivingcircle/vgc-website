Our mission is to invest in local non-profit organizations that focus on providing education and empowerment for women and girls in Los Angeles.

Who We Are

VGC is a student-initiated, donor-advised charitable fund composed of 10 juniors and 10 seniors. Being a board member is a two-year commitment that alternates between fundraising and granting.

Meet the board

What We Do

Through intensive research and constant engagement, VGC identifies the most pressing issues faced by women and girls in our community and honors the invaluable work being done to combat these challenges.

Our work
Over $500,000 granted to Los Angeles organizations serving women and girls since 2006
